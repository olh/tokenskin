🎮 CONTROLS

• WASD / Arrow Keys: Walk around the open world

• Shift: Sprint (consumes Stamina)

• Space: Jump / Swim upwards in water

• Left Click: Swing weapon, attack hostile goblins/monsters, or mine targeted blocks

• Right Click: Place blocks from hotbar, consume potions/apples, or interact with Sage Elder Bob

• V: Toggle First-Person & Third-Person Camera (RuneScape style)

• B: Toggle Battle Royale Arena Mode (Storm circle, supply chests, survival loop)

• E: Open 28-Slot Inventory

• Q: Open Quest Journal

• 1 - 9 / Mouse Wheel: Select Hotbar active slot

⚔️ MMORPG GAMEPLAY

Chop trees to train Woodcutting. Mine stone and gold/diamond ores to train Mining. Slay hostile goblins and skeletons to train Combat and Defense. Talk to Elder Bob near the shrine to advance through storyline quests.
